Most pages list dictionary entries. Each entry has a Kabalay headword, optional
grammatical markers (N. = noun, V. = verb, etc. — see "Signes et Abréviations" below),
and a French definition or translation. Some pages (cover, title page, introduction,
table of contents, "Signes et Abréviations") carry document-level information instead
of entries.

CRITICAL — merging multi-line entries: an entry's French definition often wraps across
several printed lines, and a word at the end of a line may be hyphenated and continue
on the next line. A new line starts a NEW entry ONLY when it begins with a new Kabalay
headword (typically followed by a colon or a grammatical marker like "V." or "N."). Any
line that continues the sentence from the entry above it — no new headword, a lowercase
continuation, a hyphenated word broken across the line break, an incomplete sentence —
belongs to the SAME entry. Merge it into that entry's "french" text (rejoining hyphenated
words into one) instead of creating a new entry with an empty "kabalay" or "french" field.
Never leave a field empty because you split a definition across entries by mistake — if
you produce an entry with an empty "kabalay" or "french", that is a bug: merge it into
the entry above instead.
